I am a second-year PhD student in **Mechanical Engineering** at the University of California, Riverside, advised by [Prof. Mingyu Cai](https://sites.google.com/view/ucr-real/home) in the REAL Lab. Before that I received my M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Virginia (advised by [Prof. Shangtong Zhang](https://shangtongzhang.github.io/)) and my B.Eng. in Electronic Information Engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (advised by [Prof. Xin Yang](https://sites.google.com/view/xinyang/home)).
{: .lead}

My research sits at the intersection of **robot learning, formal methods, and motion planning**. I am especially interested in making symbolic specifications, in particular **spatio-temporal logic**, *differentiable*, so that geometric and temporal task structure can be plugged directly into reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and policy evaluation pipelines.

## Research interests

- Differentiable spatio-temporal logic for robot manipulation
- Reinforcement learning and imitation learning with symbolic structure
- Motion planning under uncertainty (conformal prediction, dynamic environments)
- Multi-robot navigation and hybrid RL–planning frameworks
- Natural language to formal task specification (NL2SpaTiaL)
